# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-06)

- First release. 23 rules for backdoors, viruses and Creator Store asset rules.
- Scan selection, scan whole place and Creator Store check.
- Open at line, Select, Disable, Delete (undo with Ctrl+Z) and Ignore for each result.
- Interpolated strings, require aliases and code hidden behind spaces are all checked. Very large scripts scan in well under a second.
- Pro edition adds auto-scan on insert, report export (text and CSV) and saved ignore lists.
